# Reconstruction check (plan step 2b)

## Summary

- **Weather window: the mean of gridMET days t−1 and t** (the public code with `lag=0`). In 41 of
  43 tiles the best weather match is a two-day mean ending on the same day t that the fire masks
  pick. Over the 19 confident tiles, (t−1, t) has the lowest MAE for every variable, e.g. wind
  speed 0.074 m/s against 0.21–0.36 for the other windows. `build_inputs`' default window is right.
- **PDSI: the latest pentad dated on or before t.** It reproduces the stored channel exactly
  (median corr 1.000, MAE 0.000), and it is the best-matching pentad in 41 of 43 tiles. The two
  exceptions are tiles whose date was not identified.
- **Node-grid anchor fix (5bef496).** It raised the median correlation for tmmn from 0.941 to 0.963,
  tmmx 0.922 to 0.944, pr 0.849 to 0.960, erc 0.881 to 0.904 and vs 0.955 to 0.963.
- **Wind direction has no convention error.** On the 12 confident tiles whose raw winds stay
  away from north, `th` matches with median MAE 1.8° and corr 0.95. Tiles with northerly wind
  inherit the plain-degree averaging artifacts that the training data also has.
- **The fire mask is the one material gap.** FIRMS Terra points rasterized to their centre pixel
  catch only about 40% of the stored MOD14A1 fire pixels (precision 0.94). Rasterizing each
  detection's footprint at half its scan × track size raises the IoU against MOD14A1 from 0.34 to
  0.63. With that mask, the model on the stored inputs scores IoU 0.209 against 0.208 for the
  original masks; centre pixels give 0.194. This rule was picked on the same 19 tiles, so it
  still needs held-out confirmation.
- **What our rebuild costs** (pooled IoU against the stored label, 19 tiles):
  - stored inputs: 0.208
  - NDVI and population filled: 0.205
  - our full rebuild: 0.201
  - our rebuild with live-lag weather (t−3, t−2): 0.186
  - persistence: 0.166
- **Caveats.**
  - Only 19 tiles have a confident date, and several are the same fire on nearby days (2015 Rough
    Fire, 2015-08-11 complex). The 24 other tiles nearly all have the same t and window but
    narrower weather-match margins.
  - All dates are 2012–2020 western-US fires.

The stored MOD14A1 masks give a model IoU of 0.208. MOD14A1 grids each swath fire pixel onto
1 km cells, and off-nadir MODIS pixels are larger than 1 km (scan up to about 4.8 km), so one
point marking one cell undercounts.
